fix(sheets): scrolling inside a sheet moved or closed the sheet

Senior report, 28 Sep 2026: "scrolling inside sheet move the sheet as
well, jumpy jumpy and inconsistent."

Measured in a phone browser before the fix. Day sheet: scrolled down,
then a drag back up moved the whole sheet 100 px while the list
scrolled under it. Holidays: a gentle drag in the list closed the
sheet.

Ionic 7's sheet gesture (ion-modal createSheetGesture.canStart) lets
the content scroll only when the drag starts inside an `ion-content`
— it checks `target.closest('ion-content')` and nothing else. GModal's
scroller was a plain div, so every drag on every sheet was taken as
"move the sheet". GModal now renders its content inside an ion-content;
the pinned head (grabber, title, Close) stays outside it and still
drags the sheet, as an iOS sheet does. The sheet is a column whose
height still follows its content up to the same ceiling, so short
sheets stay short.

Every sheet in the app renders through GModal, so this is one fix for
all of them. After: the day sheet scrolls (250 -> 18) and stays put;
Holidays stays open. iOS gate: 9 of 9 audits, 0 findings, including
sheet-consistency (dark and light) and sheet-shift.
