fix(overtime): rest-day overtime is paid in half-hours, like weekdays

The owner's rest-day claim (26 Sep, 8h 52m 37s) showed 8.876944444
hours. A weekday claim is banded to HR's 30-minute pay rule (0-29 min
down, 30-49 to the half hour, 50 and up to the hour); a rest-day or
public-holiday claim skipped it and was kept exact to the second. The
question was raised on 8 Sep and never ruled. Owner ruling, 27 Sep:
"round like weekday".

The claim's holiday part now passes through the same rounding as the
weekday part, in get_ot_claim_capacity, which sizes the form, the
claimable-days list and the save cap alike, so all three agree. Pay is
priced from the approved claimed hours, so it follows. The worked
record keeps every minute; only what is claimed and paid is banded. A
day worked partly as weekday and partly as rest day bands each part
on its own.

Rest day 10:08-13:22 now claims 3.0 h (was 3.233); the owner's day
claims 9.0 h. Four tests that pinned the exact figure are updated to
the ruling; three new ones pin it. Proven on the bench in a savepoint.
