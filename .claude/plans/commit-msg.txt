fix(approvals): a request stuck when its first approver had left

Overtime, leave and other requests sat on nobody's Approvals page when
the approver named on the employee's record (or their manager) had left
or had their login disabled. The approval line already passes over that
person to the next one up, and that person could open and approve the
request - but the page and Home's count asked "was it sent to me?" from
the record fields only, so it was sent to someone who can't log in.

The page now also counts the line's first approver as "sent to".
Backups higher up still are not sent it (owner ruling 8 Oct).
