fix(requests): a waiting request could still be changed after sending

The owner opened his waiting overtime request and could change its
date, hours and note (the date sat in an editable box). Frappe lets
the owner edit a request until it is decided, because "Waiting" is
an unsubmitted draft, and the app followed Frappe. Owner ruling
(27 Sep): once sent, a request is not changed in the app. To change
one, withdraw it and send a new one.

Every request form (time off, overtime, fix a day, shift, expense,
issue) is now read-only once sent, never offers Save, and the request
sheet offers Withdraw without Edit. The approver is unaffected: they
decide through the review sheet, which still shows. A new request is
still fully editable. HR's Desk is out of scope and unchanged.
