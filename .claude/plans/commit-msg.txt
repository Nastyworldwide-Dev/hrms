feat(roster): HR can see how each person is really rostered

The roster is a new feature and the owner ruled out designing it on
guesses. Nothing on hand says how many people work one fixed shift, a
weekly rota, a rotation or day by day: the test sites hold only test
shifts. This read-only report sorts each active employee by their last
full weeks of real Shift Assignments, says who set them (by hand, a
weekly schedule or a location rule), and counts each pattern, so the
roster design starts from the live numbers.

HR roles only, fenced to the caller's companies inside the query.
Linked next to Unclaimable Days by an idempotent patch, as that one was.
