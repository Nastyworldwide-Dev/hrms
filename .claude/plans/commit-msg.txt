fix(pwa): drop nativeEl()'s wrapper fallback in GPullRefresh

frappe-reviewer, reviewing 8009391fe: nativeEl() fell back to
refresherEl.value itself when .$el was absent, which would silently
attach the listener to the Vue component wrapper instead of a DOM
node if Ionic's Vue binding ever returned a non-wrapped ref. Dropped
the `?? refresherEl.value` fallback to match ListView.vue's plain
`content.value?.$el` and let the existing `if (!el)` guard do its
job instead of masking a bad ref.

Rebuilt and reran the full suite (1513 tests) after the change: all
green, including the ionStart/ionRefresh addEventListener assertions
in pull-refresh-events-real.test.js and pull-refresh-label-resets.test.js.
