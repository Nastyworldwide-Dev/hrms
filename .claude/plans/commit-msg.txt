fix(forms): a picker's value was cut and a stray box sat after Hours

Found in the 27 Sep sweep on the installed-iPhone profile.

Issue form: "What are you reporting?  Requirec". The row label's rule
let it grow (flex 1 0 auto) to 237 pt for 193 pt of text, leaving the
picker 89 pt for a 95 pt value. The chevron is drawn inside that box,
so it covered the last letter. A label is now its text; the picker
reserves its chevron and ends a too-long value in an ellipsis.

Overtime form: "Hours  Required" plus a small block. WebKit draws a
number field's spin buttons; they were hidden only on read-only rows.
iOS has no stepper there, the keypad is the control, so they are gone
from every form number field.
