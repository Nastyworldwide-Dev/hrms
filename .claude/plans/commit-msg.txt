fix(rows): a long manager name wrapped into three short lines

On You, the owner's manager name wrapped into three right-aligned
lines beside "Manager". A label/value row always laid the two side by
side, so a long value was squeezed into whatever width the label left.
iOS does it differently: side by side only while both fit on one line,
otherwise the value goes under its label, leading-aligned.

Rows now measure their text and choose. It is on every read-only
label/value row: You, every form field, the late check-out and
remote check-in sheets. Proven with a 36-character manager name on
the installed-iPhone profile. The dead-code audit now knows global
directives the way it knows global components.
