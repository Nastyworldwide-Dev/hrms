fix(calendar): team line said "not in yet" for people already in

Owner report, 28 Sep 2026, with screenshots: at 10:25 a senior opened
today on the Calendar and read "Your team · 6 not in yet", while the
Team page behind it listed all six Present with their IN times.

The two screens answered "who is in" with two different rules. The
Team page reads punches, approved leave and the member's clock
(derive_member_status). The Calendar's _coverage counted Attendance
rows only — and auto-attendance writes those after the shift, so
every punched-in person was "unmarked" until the job ran. Not a
regression: it counted that way from the day it was written
(bc896836f). Its "on leave" count had the same gap (Attendance status
instead of the approved leave), and its idea of "my team" was a second
list that could drift from the Team page's.

Now one rule: get_team_status's body is member_statuses() and
own_team_members(); the day sheet calls both and counts the same rows,
and carries them as `team` for the names on the sheet. Owner Q3: the
manager's whole reporting line on both screens.

Proved on the site, synthetic rows rolled back: HEAD gives sheet
present 0 / unmarked 3 against the page's Present 2; after, both 2/1.
