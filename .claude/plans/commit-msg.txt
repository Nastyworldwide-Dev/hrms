fix(pwa): "A new version is ready" kept appearing with nothing new

Owner, 28 Sep 2026: the new-update popup "keeps appearing
inappropriately". Two causes, both reproduced in a real browser on the
dev site before the fix.

1. It showed with no deploy at all. Since alpha.12 main.js registers
   /hrms/sw.js — with ?config=… when push notifications are set up —
   while UpdatePrompt registered it again through vite-plugin-pwa as
   plain /hrms/sw.js. One scope, two URLs: every load installed the
   other one as "waiting", and the bar came up on 4 of 4 loads with
   nothing deployed.

2. Dismissing it saved nothing. The dismissal was keyed on a
   __WB_REVISION__ read from the worker's URL, which /hrms/sw.js has
   never carried, and it was read at registration, before anything
   was waiting. localStorage held null after a dismissal and the bar
   came back on every reload.

Now there is one registration: main.js shares it
(data/swRegistration.js) and the prompt watches it, offering only a
worker waiting behind an active one. The waiting build names itself:
public/sw.js answers GET_BUILD_ID with a hash of its own precache list
(utils/buildId.js), so a dismissal is remembered for exactly that
build. waitingBuildId is removed; workbox-window leaves the bundle.

Checked on fresh.local, push off and push on: no bar on three loads
with nothing deployed; the bar after a deploy; dismissing stores the
build id and two reloads stay clear; a newer deploy is offered again;
Reload takes it and clears the memory.
