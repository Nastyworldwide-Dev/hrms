fix(holidays): a range across two calendars counted holidays outside it

Review of 3722a3ace: when the two ends of a date range resolve to
different calendars, get_holiday_dates_between_range split at the
second calendar's start. A calendar from the Employee or Company
record usually starts on 1 Jan, long before the range, so the second
part counted holidays before start_date and the first part ran
backwards. The split point is now clamped to the range.
