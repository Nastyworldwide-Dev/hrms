feat(geofence): HR can let a person check in at more than one site

HR, 28 Sep 2026: a tickbox for people who work at more than one site.
Until now every fence reader was hard-wired to one site per person —
the Employee's Shift Location or the assignment's — so someone who
works at Damansara and Shah Alam was sent to an approver every day at
the second one.

Owner's rulings: several sites per person, set on the Employee record;
inside any of them is accepted and the site is recorded; outside all
of them follows today's rule measured from the NEAREST site (normal ->
approver, strict -> refused). The tickbox only adds places to be; it
never loosens the rule.

Employee gets "Can check in at more than one site" and "Other sites"
(new child doctype Employee Other Site), brought to existing sites by
bumping sync_custom_fields_with_code. The rule lives in one place:
employee_sites() lists the main site then the others, and
evaluate_sites() runs the existing evaluate_geofence per site. The
enforcing insert, the strict preflight and the PWA map all call it, so
what warns and what enforces cannot disagree. Employee Checkin records
"Checked in at".

Kept apart on purpose: the automatic shift rules still read the Shift
Location alone (a test locks it); not ticked = exactly today (tests);
a free-location site still means anywhere. The hub sync cannot clobber
the fields: after cutover it leaves existing Employees alone.

Proved on fresh.local with two synthetic sites 5.5 km apart, rolled
back: HEAD sends a ticked person at the second site to approval
(Outside, 5551 m); now it is Inside, "Checked in at: ProbeSite B".
Not ticked still goes to approval; far from both still goes to
approval, measured from the nearer site.

Also: hrms/api/test_remote_checkin.py was red on HEAD (12 of 73) — its
fake check-in had no .get(), which punch() has read since bcfc8a425.
The commit gate runs it as a dependent of this change, so the fake now
reads like a Document; the same 12 fail on HEAD without this change.
