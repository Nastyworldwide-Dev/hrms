fix(requests): "pull down to try again" did not reload the balances

Design review of 681545ac0. The new "Couldn't load your leave. Pull
down to try again." row, and the older "Your balances could not be
loaded. Pull down to try again." banner, both promise a pull that
reloaded only the request lists, never the balances. The pull now
reloads both. The row is also role="status", so a screen reader hears
it appear, as it already hears "Loading your balances".
