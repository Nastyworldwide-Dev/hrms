fix(roster): a Shift Supervisor could not assign shifts to their own team

HR, 30 Sep 2026: "dia tak boleh assign shift untuk budak dia". Team
roster listed a supervisor's reports by Reports To, in any company,
then Assign refused them: "You are not permitted to roster this
employee." The write also demanded the report's company be inside the
supervisor's Company User Permission, so a supervisor locked to one
company whose report sits in another could see them but not roster
them. Three separate checks said no in turn: the roster fence, the
Employee read, and Frappe's User Permission on the new Shift
Assignment.

Owner rulings the same day: "Reports To wins" and "allow self". One
list, hrms.hr.utils.rostered_employees (self + Active Reports To,
Shift Supervisor role required), now decides both the roster write and
the shift row scope, so the list and the save cannot disagree again.
Strangers stay refused; without the role nobody is rostered. The Team
roster lists the supervisor first, as "You".

Verified on fresh.local with a supervisor locked to another company:
their report and themselves saved, a stranger refused (5-case matrix).
