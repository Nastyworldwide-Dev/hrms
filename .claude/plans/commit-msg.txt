fix(security): the last person's page stayed on a shared phone

alpha.12 made the installed app open offline by keeping a copy of the
/hrms page in the "nadi-pages" cache. Frappe renders that page per
user (it carries their boot data), and nothing ever deleted it. On a
shared phone, the next person could open the previous one's page
while offline.

The copy is now deleted at logout and at login; a session that merely
expired never passes through logout. Proven in Chromium with the
built app: before logout the cache held /hrms/home for the employee;
after logout it holds only the page Frappe rendered for Guest.
