feat(more): TruTrip shortcut for business travel

Owner's boss, 28 Sep 2026: TruTrip (app.trutrip.co) needs its own icon
and button on More as a shortcut.

It can't go into the existing Apps list: those are same-origin apps
that share the Frappe session, and isSameOriginPath refuses any other
host on purpose, so a later edit can't turn a nav row into an open
redirect. TruTrip gets its own small list (data/externalLinks.js):
https only, exact-host allowlist, opened in a new tab with
noopener,noreferrer so the travel site gets no handle back to the app
and no session of ours. The same-origin guard is unchanged and a test
pins that it still refuses TruTrip's URL.

On More it is a "Travel" group, plane icon, arrow-out badge like the
other rows that leave the app. Offered to everyone.

Checked on fresh.local as an employee: the tap opened
app.trutrip.co/v2/login in a new tab, window.opener was null, and the
app stayed on More.
