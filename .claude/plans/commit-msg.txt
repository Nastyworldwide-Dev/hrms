feat(apps): an "Approva User" role that opens Approva and nothing else

Owner, 30 Sep 2026: some people need Approva (purchase requests and
approvals) without the accounting or HR access that comes with the
roles which offered it until now. Those roles keep Approva; this is
the special case.

A patch creates the role with no Desk access and no doctype rights.
Holding it only makes Nadi show the Approva link. Approva itself lets
any signed-in person open it and send a request (checked in its repo:
approva/www/approva.py and api.submit_request only require a login);
approving still needs Reporting Manager or being the named approver.

Verified on fresh.local: an employee sees no apps, with the role sees
Approva, with it removed sees none again; the patch runs twice clean.
