test(gates): measure Nadi as it is installed on an iPhone

Every gate ran a 402x874 window with no safe areas, no standalone
mode and every page opened cold from its URL. The owner's phone showed
defects none of them could see: the title doubled after coming back
to a tab, a band of chrome above every page, the tab bar near the
home indicator.

The new audit serves the CSS with the iPhone 16 Pro insets written in
and walks the app as a person does: each tab, scrolled, half-scrolled,
a pushed screen and Back. The page starts below the status bar because
index.html uses the "default" status-bar style; a test pins that
setting, since a first run that assumed otherwise blamed the app for a
gap the phone never has.

On HEAD it reported the double title on all five tabs and a 21 pt
band above every large title. Those fixes follow separately.
