fix(lists): empty lists said the same thing six different ways

Titles varied ("No leave taken this year" on a list not limited to a
year, "Nothing here yet", "No shift requests yet") and bodies pointed
at a button elsewhere ("Use New above", "tap New") or at nothing.
Apple's empty view is the thing's symbol, a title that states what is
true, one short line saying what to do, and the action itself.

Every list now reads "No <things> yet" and a short line, and an empty
list you can add to shows the New button in place.
