fix(header): coming back to a tab showed its title twice

Opening a screen from a tab (the bell, a row) and coming Back left the
bar's small title AND the large title showing, on every tab. Ionic
hides the tab page while the pushed screen is up. A hidden page reports
0x0 boxes, which the title observer read as "the large title went up
under the bar", and it never fired again on return because nothing
crossed. The installed-iPhone journey caught it on all five tabs.

Only a measured page decides now; a hidden page keeps its state.
