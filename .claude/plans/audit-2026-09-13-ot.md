## The precision row is FALSE — rewrite it

Bench schema: working_hours, ot_hours, ot_rate_weighted_hours, Attendance Overtime Band.hours,
OT Request.claimed_hours, OT Request.punch_ot_hours are all **decimal(21,9)**, not (21,2).
9dp works end to end (a holiday remainder returned 1.066666667 exactly). **1 minute does NOT become 0.02.**

The SYMPTOM is still real from a different cause: `round_ot_pay_hours` (ot_calculation.py:63-79) drops
0-29 min to :00 — HR's stated 30-minute pay band, working as designed. So the claim is refused with
"your check-outs prove at most 0.0 hours", which misrepresents a POLICY rule as an EVIDENCE failure.
Not a pay defect; it IS a wrong-message defect, same class as O3.

CAVEAT: that is the BENCH schema. Production was never migrated. Confirm patches
`check_ot_hour_precision_capacity` and `verify_ot_hour_precision` (patches.txt:2,8) actually ran on
Verifica before closing the row.

  shift_type.py:275 guards ONLY start_time, and only while punches are UNLINKED — which historical days

  the LIVE config and discards the punch snapshot, so get_ot_pay re-prices any past month from today's
