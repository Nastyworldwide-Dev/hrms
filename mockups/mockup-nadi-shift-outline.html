How it behaves

  • Filled share = time since the shift started ÷ shift length, worked out from the start time (never counted up), updated once a minute.
  • Shift complete: one light sweeps round the outline, once. That is the reward: for finishing, not for staying.
  • Past the shift end: the outline turns orange and breathes slowly; the card says "Still working? Check out when you leave."
  • Three hours past: it says you may have forgotten, and offers "I left earlier · fix the time" (the existing forgot-to-check-out request).
  • Nothing here says overtime or pay: overtime counts only when approved and claimed, so the card never promises it.
  • Reduce Motion on: no sweep, no breathing, the outline just shows its value.
