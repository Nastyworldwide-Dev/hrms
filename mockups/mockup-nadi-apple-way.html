The check-in card, the Apple way

Built from Apple's own patterns: a gauge (a progress bar with its ends labelled), a live time, and short icon animations. Each moves once, for a reason, and all of them stop when Reduce Motion is on.

What moves, and Apple's reason

  • Check in: a spinner inside the button while it saves, then a tick draws itself, and the card turns into "Working" (Apple: Draw On, content replace).
  • Working: the green dot breathes softly, like a recording indicator (Apple: Breathe, "an activity is taking place").
  • The time rolls when a minute passes, the way the Clock app's digits change.
  • The gauge fills from 09:00 to 18:00 and turns orange past the end (Apple gauges: "change the fill colour when the value reaches significant levels").
  • Forgot? The line wiggles once so it's not missed (Apple: Wiggle, "a call to action a person might overlook").
  • New notification: the bell bounces once (Apple: Bounce, "an action occurred").

Left out, on purpose

  • Rings (Apple reserves them for Fitness), confetti, sounds, looping glows, anything that celebrates overtime.
