# Shipped vs mockup 4 — an honest gap list

23 September 2026, against the deploy the owner photographed plus the
fixes made this morning. Mockup 4 is `Nadi PWA UI UX 2.0/nadi-2.0-mockup-4.html`
(1,460 lines, 11 screens, 5 sheets).

The owner's instruction: *"mockup 4 design are the way how we want it should
look... but final design must overcome what is wrong in mockup 4."* So each row
below says which of the two is right, not just which is different.

---

## The honest headline

**The shipped app follows mockup 4's SHELL and not its CONTENT.**

What matches: the tab bar and its five destinations, the glass treatment, the
typeface, the dark stage, the calendar's colour legend, the day sheet as the
centrepiece, the balance cards, the status pills, the icon system, the 44px
targets, the skip link, one `<h1>` per screen.

What does not: almost every screen has FEWER, THINNER sections than mockup 4
draws, and the difference is not styling — it is that mockup 4 shows data this
app does not yet compute.

That is the real answer to "does it follow mockup 4". It follows the design.
It does not yet carry the content the design was drawn around.

## What is wrong in mockup 4 (and was right to overcome)

1. **The four stacked request lists on Calendar.** Mockup 4 does not have
   them — its own flow map said delete — but the SHIPPED app had them until
   today. Not a mockup fault; a fault in following it.
2. **Fabricated data everywhere.** Every number in mockup 4 is invented. Six
   of its blocks need doctypes or endpoints that do not exist. That is a
   mockup's job, but it means "follow mockup 4" cannot mean "look like it"
   without building those first.
3. **"Coming up at work"** would need a company-events doctype. The
   announcement board's `Event` category is the nearest thing and is not the
   same thing.
4. **The live clock** re-renders the top of Home every second. Mockup 4 pays
   nothing for that; a real app pays a render.
5. **Eleven AA failures** in mockup 1's palette which mockup 4 itself fixed —
   the shipped tokens already carry those fixes.

## The ranked list, by how much it would change what the owner sees

1. **Requests rows say who and since when** — pure copy over data we already
   have. Biggest gain per line changed.
2. **Requests split into "Waiting on someone" / "Finished"** — no new data.
3. **Requests filter chips** — no new data.
4. **Home: the claimable-money row** (`3h 30m · RM 248 owed`) — the number is
   already computed for the Requests strip.
5. **Home: approver rows carry names and avatars** — `needs_you` returns counts;
   it would have to return rows.
6. **Calendar: "Ready" / "Not yet" grouping** — the claimable summary already
   separates these.
7. **Home: geofence state before the tap** — the endpoint exists.
8. **Score: goals with target vs actual** — needs the goals payload.
9. **Calendar: roster tab** — needs `calendar.roster`, per-persona.
10. **Home: "Coming up at work"** — needs a new doctype.
11. **Announcements as a card with a body preview** — payload change only.

1 through 4 are a morning's work and change every screen the owner opens.
