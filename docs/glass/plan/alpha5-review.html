Your bugs, answered

“Today didn’t show as present” Bug · display only
What happened

Your check-in and check-out are saved and safe. The Calendar colours a day only after the attendance record exists, and that record is made later. Home already counts today, so the two screens disagree.

Fix

Checked in and out = Worked right away. Still checked in = In progress. One shared rule for Home, Calendar and Requests.

AttendanceCalendar.vue:113 · api/__init__.py:362 · home.py:43

“1 day with no attendance: which date? what to fix?” Bug · false alarm
What happened

That “1 day” was today. The count includes today even before the day is processed. It never names the date, and tapping it opens a blank form.

Fix

Never count today. Name the day: “Tue 16 Sep has no attendance”. Tapping it opens that day with a Fix this day button.

requests_summary.py:162 · calendar.py:283 · RequestBalances.vue:212

“in an hour” on a notification Bug · wrong clock
What happened

The app never gets the site’s time zone, so it assumes Dubai, 4 hours behind Malaysia. Only 3 screens use the site clock at all. The rest use the phone’s clock.

Fix

Send the site time zone at startup, and read every server time through it.

hrms/www/hrms.py:24 · utils/siteTime.js:15 · NowBar.vue:77

Evidence

KeyRuleSource
E1“Liquid Glass forms a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation… Don’t use Liquid Glass in the content layer.”Apple HIG · Materials
E2“Always avoid glass on glass.”WWDC25 · Get to know the new design system
E3The tab bar floats above content. Use a scroll edge effect under floating bars, “one per view”. Stay inside the safe area.HIG Tab bars · Scroll views · Layout
E4Sheets: medium and large heights, a grabber, the title in the top bar, Close at the trailing edge.HIG Sheets · Toolbars
E5“Prefer brief titles that people can read at a glance.” A feed item shows who and what, then the time. No raw IDs.HIG Notifications · Microsoft Teams activity feed
E6Show a few first (progressive disclosure). Endless scroll hides the end.NN/g Progressive disclosure · NN/g Infinite scrolling
E7Empty states explain why, and what to do next.NN/g Empty states
E8A segmented control has at most about 5 segments.HIG Segmented controls
E9Always keep people informed about what is going on.NN/g Visibility of system status
E10Plain language, lower mental effort, 50–75 characters per line.NN/g Cognitive load · HIG Writing · Baymard
E1144×44 pt targets; 4.5:1 contrast; text over busy backgrounds fails.HIG Accessibility · WCAG 1.4.3 · 2.5.8
E12Honour Reduce Transparency and Reduce Motion, with a solid fallback.MDN
E13One navigation bar style; a large title; titles under 15 characters.HIG Toolbars

Honest gaps: no official guideline requires grouping notifications into “Today / Yesterday / Earlier”; it is what iOS, Gmail and Teams all do. There is no official limit on filter chips. The Material 3 pages could only be read from search results.
